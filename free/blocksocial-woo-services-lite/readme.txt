Free gift wrapping and local pickup for your WooCommerce checkout. Upgrade to Pro for express, carrier choice, radius delivery and role-based fees.

== Description ==

BlockSocial Extra Services Lite lets your customers add **gift wrapping** (with
an optional gift message) and choose **local pickup** at checkout. Selecting
pickup automatically sets the shipping cost to zero. Fees are always calculated
server-side, never trusted from the browser.

Available in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese and
Polish.

= Free features =

* Gift wrapping with a configurable fee and optional customer message
* Local pickup, with or without a fee; removes shipping cost when selected
* Enable/disable each option, set fees, tax and labels
* Show the options on the cart, the checkout, or both
* Choices saved on the order and shown in order details and emails
* HPOS compatible; secure (nonces, capability checks, server-side pricing)

= Upgrade to Pro =

[BlockSocial Extra Services Pro](https://blocksocial.eu/woo-extra-services) adds:

* Express & priority delivery tiers
* Carrier choice (e.g. DHL instead of DPD) with surcharges
* Local delivery by distance (km radius tiers)
* Unlimited custom services (flat, percent or per item)
* Per-role visibility for dropshipping / wholesale fees
* Invoice support (incl. Germanized) and automatic updates

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin to `/wp-content/plugins/` or install it from the Plugins screen.
2. Activate it (WooCommerce must be active).
3. Configure under WooCommerce → Extra Services.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does local pickup remove shipping costs? =

Yes. When a customer selects pickup, the shipping rates are replaced by a single
free pickup option.

= Can I translate it? =

It ships with eight languages and follows your site language. Additional
languages can be added via the included `languages/bwes-lite.pot` template.
